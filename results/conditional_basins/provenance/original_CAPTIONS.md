# English caption drafts

**Conditional basin comparison.** Conditional numerical resynchronization maps of the full GFM model for uniform active-power filter time constants of 1.16, 1.18, and 1.20 s. The horizontal coordinate is the equal-weight mean internal phase of converters at buses 2, 3, and 7 relative to the postfault stable equilibrium; the vertical coordinate is their mean internal frequency deviation from 60 Hz. Remote internal phases and active-power filter states are shifted uniformly while the other 46 differential states are initialized at their postfault equilibrium values. Algebraic states are reinitialized consistently. Dots denote simulated initial conditions; colors indicate finite-time outcomes. Uniformly colored triangles interpolate matching vertex classifications, whereas hatching denotes mixed vertex outcomes. Dashed curves are interpolated transition estimates, not verified separatrices. Black curves show physical postfault trajectories following a 0.15-s Bus-4 shunt fault cleared by shunt removal and opening line 4–5. Stars mark the stable equilibrium; hollow diamonds and circles indicate projections of the full saddle and actual clearing states. Physical trajectories need not remain on the initialization section. Negative rotation is established over the stated finite observation interval, not asymptotically.

**Local views.** Enlarged views of the equilibrium neighborhood (top) and clearing-state neighborhood (bottom), using the same coordinates, classification rules, and symbols as the comparison map. The equilibrium and saddle projections remain fixed across the time-constant settings, while the numerical recovery classification changes. Background classifications apply only to the specified initial-state section.

**Hidden-state sensitivity.** Internal-angle responses from identical projected initial coordinates but different values of the remaining 46 differential states. The horizontal axis is time from the constructed initial state; the vertical axis is the equal-weight mean internal phase of buses 2, 3, and 7 relative to the postfault stable equilibrium, divided by 2π. Solid curves initialize the held states at the equilibrium; dashed curves use the physical clearing-state values. Only the first 100 s are displayed; the classifications of these differing-outcome pairs are checked over equal 1000-s intervals. This comparison demonstrates that the two plotted coordinates alone do not uniquely determine recovery in the full model.
